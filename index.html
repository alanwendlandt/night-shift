How to play

One wave at a time

  1. Stay moving. Defeat the wave; every fifth round brings a boss.
  2. Save ammo. An empty gun switches to a loaded one automatically. Use melee if all guns are empty. Melee finishing blows earn 20% more cash and points.
  3. Restock between rounds. Spend cash, then click Start Next Round.
Watch the map warning

Wire activates at unpredictable intervals with a short warning, surf slows, fog drifts, and hospital lights fail every fifth round.

Gray: ammo · Blue: power-up · Gold: map special

MAX AMMO fully refills every owned gun (rare: at most one drop per round). Drops blink before disappearing.

Weapon forge & supplies

In Workshop, each owned gun has three paid tiers with special rounds and a full ammo refill. Deploy a turret with T or its button; purchases are unlimited, with up to three deployments per round and three active at once. Surviving turrets stay on the field; destroyed turrets do not refund a deployment. PURGE clears zombies inside the arena. LETHAL ROUNDS makes every attack a one-hit kill for 12 seconds, including bosses.

Special waves

Special waves appear on every fourth round except boss rounds: runner rush, armored patrol, and fragile swarms rotate. The shop previews the next wave. Chargers warn before charging; spitters hiss before firing.

A second chance

Second chance kits cost $650 and can be bought only once per run; they restore 60% health and pause the action. Click Continue round to resume with four seconds of protection.

Local best round, kills, and score are tracked separately for every map. Power countdowns use the fixed strip below the arena.
